Find Help

Get assistance from motivated teen volunteers - completely free.

We connect you with responsible, supervised teen volunteers at no cost to you.

How It Works

Simple, safe, and free

Our matching system connects you with volunteers based on your location and the type of help you need. Every volunteer is supervised by a parent or guardian, and all tasks are pre-approved.

Important to know

  • All services are completely free
  • Volunteers are minors (ages 13-18) providing non-professional assistance
  • Parent or guardian supervision required for all activities
  • Tasks must be safe, age-appropriate, and pre-approved
  • You will receive a tracking code to confirm completed hours
Who We Help

Busy Families

Yard cleanup, organizing, homework help for younger kids, pet care while you are at work.

Seniors

Light yard work, organizing, tech help, companionship, and basic errand preparation.

Parents

Tutoring for younger children, organizing kids rooms, pet care while you are busy.

Everyone

Seasonal yard cleanup, garage organization, general errands, and other approved tasks.
