📋 About Our Hour Verification

We provide verified documentation that most schools accept as independent community service.

Our hour verification process includes:

  • ✓ Digital tracking of all volunteer hours
  • ✓ Confirmation from service recipients
  • ✓ Adult advisor oversight and review
  • ✓ Official verification letters with organization letterhead
  • ✓ Detailed service records (dates, tasks, hours completed)
  • ✓ Independent verification available upon request

Important: While we cannot guarantee acceptance by every institution, our documentation meets the standard requirements for verified community service hours. Schools, clubs, and scholarship programs typically accept hours that are unpaid, community-focused, and verifiable by an organization with adult oversight.

Nature of Volunteer Work

All work is unpaid, non-professional, and age-appropriate. Approved tasks include yard work, organizing, tutoring, pet care, and light cleaning. We do NOT provide on-site supervision.

Parent/Guardian Responsibilities

  • Ensure transportation to/from locations
  • Know where volunteer is working
  • Give permission before accepting opportunities
  • Volunteer may decline any task if uncomfortable

Prohibited Activities - Volunteers will NOT:

  • Use power tools, high ladders, or machinery
  • Handle hazardous materials
  • Provide professional services
  • Work in unsafe conditions
  • Accept payment
