About Us

We are a youth-led volunteer organization dedicated to connecting motivated teenagers with local families who need help with everyday tasks. Our goal is to create opportunities for teens (ages 13+) to gain real-world experience, build responsibility, and earn verified community service hours—while making a meaningful impact in their communities.

Every day, many families—especially elderly individuals, busy parents, and those in need of extra support—struggle to keep up with simple but important tasks. At the same time, many teens are looking for ways to get involved, give back, and build skills for the future. Our organization bridges that gap.

We match teen volunteers with safe, age-appropriate service opportunities such as tutoring, yard work, organizing, and more. All work is completely voluntary and unpaid, and every hour is tracked and verified to ensure it qualifies as legitimate community service.

We are committed to maintaining a safe, structured, and transparent system, with parent consent, clear guidelines, and reliable hour verification. By doing this, we aim to empower the next generation while strengthening trust and connection within our community.

Become a Volunteer

Join our community of teen volunteers and start making a difference in your neighborhood while earning verified service hours.

Volunteering with us is more than just fulfilling service hour requirements—it's about building real-world skills, gaining confidence, and making genuine connections in your community. Whether you're looking to strengthen your college applications, explore different career paths, or simply give back, we provide structured, safe opportunities that fit your schedule and interests.

As a volunteer, you'll be matched with families and individuals in your area who need help with tasks that match your skills and availability. All work is supervised by parents or guardians, completely voluntary and unpaid, and every hour you complete is tracked and verified for official documentation.

Verified Hours

Every hour you volunteer is digitally tracked and verified. We provide official documentation that can be used for school requirements, scholarships, college applications, and honor societies.

Real Experience

Gain practical skills in communication, responsibility, time management, and problem-solving. Build your resume while discovering potential career interests.

Flexible Schedule

Choose opportunities that fit your availability. Volunteer on weekends, after school, or during breaks—it's entirely up to you and your family.

Find Help

Get assistance from responsible, motivated teen volunteers in your community—completely free.

We understand that everyday tasks can become overwhelming, whether you're a busy parent, an elderly individual, or someone who needs extra support. Our program connects you with reliable teen volunteers who are eager to help—at no cost to you.

Every volunteer is supervised by their parent or guardian, and all tasks are pre-approved to ensure they're safe and age-appropriate. Our matching system connects you with volunteers based on your location, the type of help you need, and their skills and availability.

How It Works

1

Submit Request

Fill out our service request form describing the task you need help with and your preferred dates.

2

Get Matched

We review your request and match you with a qualified volunteer in your area based on skills and availability.

3

Connect

You'll receive the volunteer's parent contact information to coordinate the details and schedule.

4

Verify Hours

After the service is completed, confirm the hours to help the volunteer earn verified community service credit.

What We Can Help With

For Busy Families

Yard cleanup, organizing cluttered spaces, helping children with homework, walking the dog, or assistance with basic household tasks.

For Seniors

Light yard work, organizing, reading assistance, companionship, help with basic errands preparation, or tech support.

For Parents

Tutoring or homework help for younger children, organizing kids' rooms or play areas, pet care while you're at work.

For Everyone

Seasonal yard cleanup, garage organization, moving assistance (light items), event setup help, or other approved tasks.

Important Information

  • All services are completely free—volunteers are unpaid and doing this for community service hours
  • All volunteers are minors (ages 13-18) providing non-professional assistance
  • Parent or guardian supervision is required for all volunteer activities
  • Tasks must be safe, age-appropriate, and pre-approved by our organization
  • You'll receive a tracking code to confirm completed hours after service

Ready to request help? Fill out our service request form and we'll match you with a volunteer.

Safety is Our Priority

We maintain strict safety protocols to protect our volunteers, families, and the integrity of our program.

Parent Consent Required

All teen volunteers must have signed parent or guardian consent before participating. We collect emergency contact information and ensure parents are aware of all volunteer activities.

Pre-Approved Tasks Only

Every service request is carefully reviewed by our team to ensure it falls within our approved categories and is safe and age-appropriate for teen volunteers.

Right to Decline

Volunteers always have the right to decline or stop any task if they feel unsafe, uncomfortable, or if the situation differs from what was described.

Clear Agreements

Both volunteers and families sign agreements outlining program rules, expectations, and liability waivers to ensure everyone understands their responsibilities.
