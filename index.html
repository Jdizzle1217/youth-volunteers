About Our Program

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

Community Youth Volunteers is a nonprofit volunteer service network dedicated to helping teenagers aged 13 and older gain meaningful community service experience while supporting busy families with everyday tasks.

All volunteer work through our program is voluntary and unpaid. Teenagers earn verified community service hours that can be used for school requirements, club participation, scholarship applications, and college admissions.

We believe in the power of service to transform both volunteers and the communities they serve. Through safe, structured opportunities, we help teens develop responsibility, confidence, and valuable life skills.

Our Service Categories

We offer safe, age-appropriate volunteer opportunities across three main categories. All tasks are pre-approved and carefully matched to volunteer skills and availability.

Yard & Home Services

  • Yard cleanup and maintenance
  • Leaf raking and debris removal
  • Light organizing projects
  • Basic cleaning assistance
  • Garage organization

Educational Support

  • Tutoring younger children
  • Homework help and guidance
  • Reading assistance
  • Study skills development
  • Test preparation support

Pet Care Services

  • Dog walking
  • Pet sitting
  • Basic pet care assistance
  • Playing with pets
  • Feeding and water duties

How It Works

Our streamlined process makes it easy for teens to start volunteering and for families to get the help they need.

1

Apply

Teens complete our volunteer application with parent or guardian consent. Families submit service requests describing their needs.

2

Match

We review all requests for safety and appropriateness, then match volunteers with opportunities based on their skills, interests, and availability.

3

Serve

Volunteers complete approved tasks while parents stay informed of their location. Families receive reliable, helpful assistance from motivated teens.

4

Verify

Hours are verified, digitally logged, and officially documented. We provide verified service hour confirmations for school and college applications.

Safety is Our Priority

We maintain strict safety protocols to protect our volunteers, families, and the integrity of our program.

Parent Consent Required

All teen volunteers must have signed parent or guardian consent before participating. We collect emergency contact information and ensure parents are aware of all volunteer activities.

Pre-Approved Tasks Only

Every service request is carefully reviewed by our team to ensure it falls within our approved categories and is safe and age-appropriate for teen volunteers.

Right to Decline

Volunteers always have the right to decline or stop any task if they feel unsafe, uncomfortable, or if the situation differs from what was described.

Clear Agreements

Both volunteers and families sign agreements outlining program rules, expectations, and liability waivers to ensure everyone understands their responsibilities.
